## Planning rules for this plan

- Calendar commitments are the source of truth for fixed commitments.
- Do not store other people's names in plans.
- Do not skip protected commitments, the sleep floor, or daily personal time.
- All-day work or job tasks from the calendar become scheduled work blocks.
- Eating times may be scheduled, but meal plans are not needed unless requested.
- Unknowns stay as `Unknown` until confirmed.
